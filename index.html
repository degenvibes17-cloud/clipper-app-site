Clipper

A personal tool for publishing short video clips to the operator's own channels.

What it does

Clipper is a private, self-hosted application used by a single operator to prepare short vertical clips from long-form video and publish them to social media accounts that the operator owns and manages. It runs on the operator's own computer; it is not a public service and does not offer accounts to other users.

How it uses Google / YouTube

When the operator connects one of their own YouTube channels, Clipper requests permission to:

  • Upload videos to that channel (YouTube Data API, youtube.upload), so a clip the operator chooses can be published or scheduled.
  • Read basic channel information (youtube.readonly), to confirm the right channel is connected and to check that a published video is public.

Every upload is started by the operator. Clipper never posts on its own, never reads or modifies other people's channels, and never shares any data with third parties.
