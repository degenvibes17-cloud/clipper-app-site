Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Clipper ("the app") is a self-hosted tool operated by a single person to publish short video clips to social media accounts that person owns. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data the app accesses

When the operator connects their own YouTube channel through Google sign-in, the app receives:

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens that allow it to upload videos to that channel and read basic channel and video information (YouTube Data API scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly).
  • The channel's ID, handle and title, used to confirm the correct channel is connected.
  • The privacy status and public statistics of videos the app uploaded, used to confirm a scheduled video went public.

When the operator connects their own TikTok account, the app receives OAuth tokens allowing it to read basic profile information (open ID and display name, scope user.info.basic) and to upload videos to that account's inbox as drafts (scope video.upload).

When the operator connects their own Instagram professional account, the app receives a token allowing it to publish Reels to that account and read the resulting post's link.

The app does not request or store email addresses, contacts, files, followers, messages, or any data about other users or other accounts.

How data is used

Data is used only to perform actions the operator starts: uploading, scheduling or sending a chosen clip to their own account and checking its status. The app does not use data for advertising, does not sell or transfer it, and does not use it to build user profiles.

Storage and security

Tokens and channel details are stored only on the operator's own computer, in a local file that is excluded from version control and never transmitted anywhere except to the relevant platform's API (Google/YouTube, TikTok or Instagram) over HTTPS. No data is stored on any server operated by the app.

Sharing

Data is not shared with any third party.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Clipper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services and is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time: for Google/YouTube at myaccount.google.com/permissions; for TikTok in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions; for Instagram in Accounts Center, Apps and websites. Disconnecting a channel in the app deletes its stored tokens from the operator's computer.
